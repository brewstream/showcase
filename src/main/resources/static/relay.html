BrewStream showcase

Relaying a stream to a player, and transcribing it as it passes
Listener Caller Relay

Two SRT listeners: an encoder publishes MPEG-TS to one, ffplay calls the other. Port 0 takes one from barista.ports.srt.

A relay is a Barista brew. One source, an SRT listener the encoder publishes to, fanned out to one output, an SRT listener the player calls. Both ends dial in, so neither needs to know where the other is.

The transcript comes from a third output on every relay, which the showcase itself calls over loopback, exactly as ffplay calls the player output. Grind finds the AAC track, JAAD decodes it to PCM, and inference4j's Moonshine streaming recognizer turns speech into lines, pushed over /ws/transcript?brew=<id>: started, revised while spoken, completed once final. ADTS AAC only (LC and HE-AAC); LATM is not supported.
