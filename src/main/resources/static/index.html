SRT transport health and MPEG-TS media health, sampled together

What to watch. Transport loss and media damage are different things. Packets lost are usually recovered by retransmission and cost nothing visible. Packets dropped are ones ARQ could not recover inside the latency budget — those are gone, and they land as continuity errors on a specific track a moment later. Raising the latency gives recovery more room; lowering it makes the damage appear.
